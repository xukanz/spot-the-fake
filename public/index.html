Spot the Fake

Draw together. Find the player with a different word.

How to play

Three to eight players each get a secret drawing word. One player gets a different but similar word, without knowing they are undercover. Take turns adding exactly one stroke in each of two rounds. Discuss the drawing, then vote for the undercover. If caught, the undercover can still win by guessing the others’ word.
